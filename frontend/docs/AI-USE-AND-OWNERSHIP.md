# AI assistance and participant ownership

This repository was developed with ChatGPT assistance, using the supplied SRS, Gemini conversation and tasksheet as review inputs. It is a learning/reference implementation and an integration starting point. It is not evidence of the participants' own completed development effort.

The supplied SRS (page 11) requires meaningful modification and understanding of AI-assisted code, acknowledgement of AI tools, and participant-authored documentation. It explicitly disallows using AI to fully produce ready-made project documentation. Do not submit these developer notes as the group's final report.

Before using this material in an assessed submission, each participant should:

1. Read their assigned modules and explain the data flow and design decisions without assistance.
2. Identify shortcuts, replace the relevant mock services and record meaningful changes in version control.
3. Write/modify tests that cover their own implementation, including failure and permission cases.
4. Review original/third-party asset licenses and replace fictional data only with approved material.
5. Write their own final report, diagrams, installation instructions and explanation of assumptions based on the system they actually built.
6. Record the AI tools actually used (including Gemini if its suggestions influenced the result), what assistance they provided, and what the participants changed and validated. Do not invent a contribution log.

Suggested oral-review questions: Why are categories and fandoms separate? Why is a route guard not authorization? How are unpublished submissions protected? Why does a user have at most one rating per item? How does a failed location request behave? What does the assistant do when it has no source? What did the tests actually exercise, and what remains untested?

There is no promise of a competition score, production readiness, full SRS compliance, or independent authorship. The group's implementation and demonstrated understanding determine those outcomes.
